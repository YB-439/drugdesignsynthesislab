About the Laboratory

Drug Design & Synthesis Lab (DDSL) • Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Drug Research

The Drug Design & Synthesis Lab (DDSL) is an academic research group operating within the Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Drug Research at Punjabi University, Patiala. Led by Prof. Yogita Bansal, the laboratory focuses on rational, computer-assisted design, organic synthesis, and pharmacological evaluation of novel heterocyclic chemical scaffolds.

Research Philosophy

Our research philosophy bridges modern in-silico computational molecular modeling (2D/3D-QSAR, docking, molecular dynamics, ADMET, and machine learning) with benchtop organic synthesis of privileged heterocyclic scaffolds (benzimidazoles, coumarins, pyrazoles, indoles, and quinazolines). This integrated approach accelerates the discovery of potent, target-selective therapeutic leads with optimized pharmacokinetic profiles.

Institutional Heritage

Established in 1962, the Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Drug Research at Punjabi University, Patiala is one of Northern India's oldest and most prestigious pharmacy schools. The department holds NAAC A+ accreditation, AICTE and PCI recognition, with modern analytical instruments and dedicated computational chemistry infrastructure.
