About the Laboratory

Drug Design & Synthesis Lab (DDSL) • Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Drug Research

The Drug Design & Synthesis Lab (DDSL) is an academic research group operating within the Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Drug Research at Punjabi University, Patiala. Led by Prof. Yogita Bansal, the laboratory focuses on rational, computer-assisted design, organic synthesis, and pharmacological evaluation of novel heterocyclic chemical scaffolds.

Research Philosophy

Our research philosophy bridges modern in-silico computational molecular modeling (2D/3D-QSAR, docking, molecular dynamics, ADMET, and machine learning) with benchtop organic synthesis of privileged heterocyclic scaffolds (benzimidazoles, coumarins, pyrazoles, indoles, and quinazolines). This integrated approach accelerates the discovery of potent, target-selective therapeutic leads with optimized pharmacokinetic profiles.

Institutional Heritage

Established in 1962, the Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Drug Research at Punjabi University, Patiala is one of Northern India's oldest and most prestigious pharmacy schools. The department holds NAAC A+ accreditation, AICTE and PCI recognition, with modern analytical instruments and dedicated computational chemistry infrastructure.

Disciplines & Domains

Core Research Focus Areas

Six key pillars defining our computational and wet-lab drug discovery programs

1. CADD & Virtual Screening

Structure-based and ligand-based virtual screening of commercially available and in-house compound libraries against validated therapeutic targets to identify promising hit scaffolds.

Focus: Lead identification • Pharmacophore mapping

2. QSAR Modeling & Machine Learning

Development of SMILES-based 2D-QSAR and 3D field point models using CORALSEA Monte Carlo correlation balance, Random Forest, XGBoost, and Support Vector Machines with strict applicability domains.

Focus: Potency regression • Isoform selectivity

3. Docking & Molecular Dynamics

Detailed receptor-ligand interaction modeling using AutoDock Vina, Lead Finder, and 100 ns+ Desmond / GROMACS simulations, followed by MM/GBSA binding free energy calculation.

Focus: RMSD / RMSF stability • Binding free energy

4. Medicinal Chemistry & Heterocyclic Synthesis

Multi-step synthetic design and preparation of novel benzimidazole, coumarin, quinazoline, and chromone hybrid scaffolds with full characterization via 1H/13C NMR and HRMS spectrometry.

Focus: Privileged scaffolds • Hybrid heterocycles

5. Structure–Activity Relationships (SAR)

Systematic substitution analysis exploring electronic, steric, and lipophilic effects of functional groups across synthetic series to establish robust empirical SAR rules.

Focus: Lead optimization • ADMET enhancement

6. Target-Specific Biological Evaluation

Enzymatic and cellular assay validation against validated therapeutic targets including BACE-1, VEGFR-2, p38α MAPK, Acetylcholinesterase (AChE), and inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, TNF-α).

Focus: In-vitro enzyme inhibition • In-vivo models

Kinase QSAR Tool v1.0.0

NIK-Predict

NF-κB Inducing Kinase (NIK / MAP3K14) Bioactivity Prediction Platform

Consensus 3-run Monte Carlo 2D-QSAR model developed within the CORALSEA-2024 platform for rapid, reliable virtual screening of small-molecule NIK inhibitors.

Input Format: SMILES strings (Batch or Single molecule)
Output: Predicted pIC50, IC50 (nM/µM), AD check, CSV Export
Validation: R² = 0.765, Q²(F1) = 0.660, CCC = 0.861, MAE = 0.291
Domain Threshold: Defect(SMILES) < 7.98 • MW ≥ 150 g/mol
Download NIK-Predict (.exe) Open Web Server →
Supported OS: Windows 10/11 (64-bit standalone portable executable, no Python required).
Isoform Selectivity JMGM 2026

p38α/β-Predict

p38 MAP Kinase α & β Isoform Selectivity & Potency Platform

Ensemble Monte Carlo 2D-QSAR model for predicting continuous inhibitory potency (pIC50) against p38α (MAPK14) and qualitative classification on p38β (MAPK11) to evaluate isoform selectivity.

Input Format: SMILES strings / Chemical structures
Endpoints: p38α pIC50 continuous + p38β binary activity (Active/Inactive)
Validation: p38α: R² = 0.871, Q² = 0.859, CCC = 0.932 | p38β: Acc = 1.00
Citation: J. Mol. Graph. Model. 143, 109246 (2026)
