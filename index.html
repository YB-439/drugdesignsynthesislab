NIK-Predict

MAP3K14 Bioactivity Predictor

Consensus 3-run Monte Carlo 2D-QSAR platform for rapid, reliable virtual screening of small-molecule NIK inhibitors. Available as a standalone offline desktop application for Windows without Python dependencies.

Model Specifications →

p38α/β-Predict

p38 MAP Kinase Potency & Selectivity

Consensus Monte Carlo QSAR ensemble evaluating continuous inhibitory potency on p38α (MAPK14) alongside qualitative classification on p38β (MAPK11) to establish isoform selectivity for inflammatory therapeutic design.

Model Specifications →
Read Article

IL-6/TNF-α-Predict

Dual Cytokine Inhibition Predictor

Consensus Monte Carlo 2D-QSAR platform for dual prediction of small-molecule inhibitory activity (pIC₅₀) against IL-6 and TNF-α pro-inflammatory cytokines in dual-target anti-inflammatory discovery.
