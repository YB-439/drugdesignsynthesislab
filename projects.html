Open Synthesis Network (OSN) Initiative
View on DNDi India Foundation

Collaborative research partnership between the Drug Design & Synthesis Lab, Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Drug Research, Punjabi University, Patiala (under the leadership of Prof. Yogita Bansal) and the DNDi India Foundation under the global Open Synthesis Network initiative through a formalized MoU signed in 2023. This initiative provides master's and undergraduate research scholars with hands-on training in synthesizing novel drug-like chemical entities targeting neglected tropical diseases, fostering critical skills in synthetic methodology and purification.
