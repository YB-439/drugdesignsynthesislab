NIK-Predict

A Bioactivity Prediction Platform for NIK Inhibitor Research

NIK-Predict is a computational tool developed at the Drug Design & Synthesis Lab (DDSL), Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Drug Research, Punjabi University, Patiala, to help researchers estimate the inhibitory activity of small molecules against NF-κB-inducing kinase (NIK/MAP3K14).

The platform brings our Monte Carlo-based 2D-QSAR model into a simple, easy-to-use application. Researchers can enter a molecular SMILES string, visualize the structure, and obtain a predicted pIC₅₀ value without running the underlying modeling workflow themselves.

Target Biology MAP3K14 Signaling Axis

NIK: A Key Target in NF-κB Signalling

NF-κB-inducing kinase (NIK), also known as MAP3K14, is a serine/threonine protein kinase that plays a key role in regulating the non-canonical NF-κB signalling pathway. This pathway is involved in several important biological processes, including immune system development, B-cell survival, and the regulation of inflammatory responses.

Under normal conditions, NIK levels are tightly controlled by cellular regulatory mechanisms. When NIK accumulates, it promotes the processing of NF-κB2 p100 into p52, contributing to the activation of downstream gene expression.

Because of its central role in this signalling pathway, NIK has attracted significant interest as a potential target for the development of small-molecule inhibitors in conditions driven by aberrant non-canonical NF-κB activation, such as autoimmune diseases, inflammatory disorders, and lymphoid malignancies.

Computational Workflow

How It Works

Seamless 3-step prediction workflow from chemical string to validated bioactivity estimation.

1

Enter a Molecule

Simply provide the molecular structure as a SMILES string (e.g., CC1=CC=C...) or upload a supported SMILES file.

2

Run the QSAR Model

NIK-Predict parses the molecular graph, calculates optimal Monte Carlo correlation balance descriptors, and processes the compound through the calibrated model.

3

Obtain Predicted pIC₅₀

Returns the model-predicted bioactivity value alongside applicability domain metrics, providing immediate insight into predicted inhibitory potency.

Methodology

About the Prediction Model

The application uses a 2D-QSAR model developed using the CORALSEA-2024 Monte Carlo approach. The model was selected following rigorous comparative evaluation of candidate QSAR models across diverse descriptor combinations and split configurations.

The Monte Carlo optimization balances correlation between graph-based structural features (local invariants, bond types, atom types) and experimental NIK inhibitory potencies (pIC₅₀ values), ensuring generalizability within the defined chemical space.

Limitations & Scope

NIK-Predict provides computational estimates of NIK inhibitory activity, and its predictions are limited by the performance of the underlying QSAR model and the chemical space covered by the training dataset.

Predictions for compounds outside the model's applicability domain may be less reliable.

Experimental validation is required to confirm the actual inhibitory activity of predicted compounds.
