NIK-Predict

A Bioactivity Prediction App for Identifying Potential NIK Inhibitors

NIK-Predict is a computational application developed by the Drug Design & Synthesis Lab (DDSL), Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Drug Research, Punjabi University, Patiala, to help researchers identify and prioritize potential inhibitors of NF-κB-inducing kinase (NIK/MAP3K14).

The application uses a 2D-QSAR model developed in CORALSEA software using the Monte Carlo algorithm to predict the inhibitory activity of small molecules against NIK. Researchers can enter a molecule's SMILES string to obtain its predicted pIC₅₀ value and assess its potential for further investigation.

Why NIK-Predict?

Predicting the activity of newly designed molecules can help researchers make more informed decisions during early-stage drug discovery. NIK-Predict provides a convenient way to explore potential NIK inhibitors and prioritize compounds for further computational or experimental evaluation.

How It Works

Simply provide the molecular structure as a SMILES string or upload a supported SMILES file. NIK-Predict processes the input and returns the model-predicted bioactivity value.

Your Data, Your Privacy

Your privacy matters to us. NIK-Predict runs locally on your computer; molecular structures and SMILES strings you submit are not stored or collected.

Data is not uploaded to any external server, ensuring complete confidentiality.
